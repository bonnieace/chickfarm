Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Use the master mascot image as
an identity reference for every subsequent asset.

warm dark eyes, feather tuft, forest-green farm dungarees with a little egg
emblem. Welcoming pose, one wing raised. Soft tactile feathers and fabric,
recognizable silhouette, exactly two wings and two feet, no human hands.
Full body, centered, transparent background, no lettering or surrounding objects.

Balanced proportions, generous padding, no extra words or scene.
