# ChickFarm brand

ChickFarm helps poultry farmers manage multiple flocks, egg production and sales,
expenses, workers, mortality and vaccinations.

## Character

A friendly golden chick in forest-green farm dungarees, with an orange beak and
feet, dark eyes and a small feather tuft. Keep two wings and two feet. The mascot
welcomes, guides and celebrates; it must never obscure farm figures or controls.
Use the master image as the identity reference for all subsequent illustrations.

## Palette

| Role | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Forest green / primary | #176B45 |
| Deep green / text | #153D2A |
| Chick gold / accent | #F7C843 |
| Warm cream / background | #FFF9ED |
| Beak orange / accent | #ED8A27 |

Use readable dark text on gold and cream. Keep critical warnings explicit.
Suggested typeface: Plus Jakarta Sans, with system sans-serif fallback.

## Asset use

- `assets/brand/chickfarm-mascot.png` — master transparent mascot: welcome, authentication and onboarding.
- `assets/brand/chickfarm-logo.png` — logo: simplified chick symbol beside the exact wordmark ChickFarm.
- `assets/brand/chickfarm-onboarding.png` — onboarding illustration: farm/flock setup with the same character.
- Social artwork: feature-focused illustration with short readable copy.

Preserve clear space around the mascot and logo. Do not stretch, recolour, add
limbs or substitute other chicken characters. Marketing mockups must not be
presented as screenshots of the actual application.
